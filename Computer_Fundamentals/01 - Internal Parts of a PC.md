# Main Parts of a PC

- **Motherboard** - Connects all the interior components, like the memory, processor, graphics card, and others.
- **CPU - Central Processing Unit** - Like the brain of the computer, it carries out instructions and performs the basic calculations needed for the system to work.
- **GPU - Graphics processing unit** - A chip designed to rapidly render graphics and display graphics and images on your screen. It is responsible for processing the graphics that are shown on the screen.
- **RAM - Random Access Memory** - The computer's fast, short-term memory. It stores the data that the computer needs right now to open apps, open files, and multitask smoothly.
- **SSD - Solid Slate Drive** - A fast computer storage device that uses memory chips to store files, games, and operating systems. It works without any moving parts.
- **HDD - Hard Disk Drive** - A traditional storage device that uses magnetic spinning disks to store and retrieve digital data from the computer.
- **PSU - Power Supply Unit** - This powers all the parts in the PC. It converts the voltage coming into the PC into the appropriate amount of electrical power needed for each of the parts to work properly.
