# What is the Internet?

The **Internet** is one giant network made up of many smaller networks connected together.

The Internet began with the **ARPANET** project in the late 1960s, funded by the **U.S. Department of Defense**. In **1989**, **Tim Berners-Lee** created the **World Wide Web (WWW)**, which made it easier to store, access, and share information online.

## Types of Networks

* **Private Network** – A network used within a home, school, or business. Devices communicate locally and are not directly accessible from the Internet.
* **Public Network** – A network that is accessible over the Internet, allowing communication between networks worldwide.

> **Tim Berners-Lee** invented the **World Wide Web (WWW)**, not the Internet.
